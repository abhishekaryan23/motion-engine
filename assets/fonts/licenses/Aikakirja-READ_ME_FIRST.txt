Information on Aikakirja Ornaments 1
General information
Dingbats or image font suited for giving your fantasy-themed products some extra glow. Originally designed for a book series by the Finnish name Aikakirjat (Chronicles). The name of the book series alludes to a special kind of people who live for hundreds, even thousands, of years. The font will be released for all enthusiasts and artists alike to honor Dieter Steffmann (Typographer Mediengestaltung in 1001fonts) who’s fonts can be accessed through 1001fonts.com and to show my respect for people involved with Birdfont, which is a software I used for the work. See birdfont.org.
So far tested only  with Finnish keyboard setup on mac. Testing and refining is to be continued as of august 2026.
On the software that made this project possible
Please try Birdfont together with another software I trusted for this work, Inkscape, if you want to create your own fonts:

Tips
This font is designed in such a way as to make good use of Mac Os Finnish – Extended keyboard setup, though there are a few ways of arriving at different characters, I didn’t employ.
I’ve made a few letters exact same, because certain keys might otherwise produce unwelcome results on my Mac. Taking this and the presence of few glyphs with no sign into account the font has almost 290 different glyphs instead of the 303 that the computer counts.
To see all of the glyphs you can use Font Book on Mac, download Character Map UWP (which I’ve not been able to test yet) for your Microsoft Windows computer or get Fontmatrix (which I’ve not been able to test either) for your computer running linux. Alternatively you can copy the list of characters at the end of this document to a text editor and choose Aikakirja Ornaments 1 font for the copied text to see all your options.
