# Music beds (0.10 Q)

Instrumental beds generated with Google Lyria 3 Pro (preview) through OpenRouter
on 2026-10-02 for MotionEngine voice-led reels (prompts: instrumental, no vocals,
even dynamics under a voice-over). Bundled in the repository as a generated library.
Redistribution confirmed by the maintainer on 2026-10-04.

`catalog.json` maps each bed to the emotions it serves; `<id>.music.json` is the
engine's MusicPlan (`motion-engine music-index`).
